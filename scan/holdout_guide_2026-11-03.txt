Busy Beaver 6, status on November 3, 2026
607 machines still stand in the way of BB(6)
BB(6) is the longest any 6-state Turing machine can run from a blank tape and still stop. To pin it down, every 6-state machine has to be shown either to halt or to run forever. These are the ones not yet settled in busycoq, the shared Rocq proof.

Search them, filter them, and open any machine to see its table and what it does.

Antihydra, the best-known holdout. Each row is the tape after one more step, from top to bottom. Filled cells are 1s, and the red line is the head.

How to read a machine
A machine is written as six groups, one per state, A to F. Each group has two moves: what to do when the head reads a 0, then when it reads a 1. A move like 1LE means write 1, step left, go to state E. --- has no move, so reaching it means the machine halts.

Every machine starts in state A on a blank tape of 0s. Machines that share a table but start in a different state, or that mirror each other, are shown as the same table. One proof often covers several.

A Bell is a burst of sweeps that grows and resets. A bouncer goes back and forth over a tape that grows steadily. A cryptid has a halting question as hard as a Collatz-style problem.

About this page
What counts as open
The list is the official holdout list of 608 machines from October 30, 2026, minus the one proved in busycoq since then (head 33209bc). A machine leaves the list only when a Rocq proof is merged, so a proof in an open PR still counts as open here.

How far to trust the notes
Nothing here is a proof unless it says proved in Rocq. Notes come from our own experiments, most of them from before the newest proofs, and some may be wrong. "Looks like a lottery" is our judgement, not a theorem. Proofs in open PRs have not been reviewed by the maintainer.

The numbers
Each machine shows its line on the 608 list and on the older 663 list, because both are in use. "Simulated" is the furthest our simulators ran it from blank with no halt, usually by jumping over repeats. It is evidence, not a proof. Reports by others come from the community sheet and Discord, as of late October and early November.

BB(6) on the wiki, holdout lists, community sheet, busycoq, open PRs. To link to one machine, add its number to this page's link, for example #m253.
